## Overview


CodeMate is the official student-led tech community of the School of Technology (SoT), North-Eastern Hill University (NEHU), Shillong. The community is established, managed, and continuously developed by students, with the objective of creating a strong ecosystem for learning, collaboration, professional growth, and career development.


CodeMate was created to bridge the gap between academic learning and industry requirements. The community helps students prepare for placements, build real-world projects, connect with mentors, explore career opportunities, and gain practical exposure to modern technologies.


The community is open to students from all colleges as well as working professionals who wish to learn, collaborate, mentor, or contribute to the growth of the ecosystem.


CodeMate provides access to educational resources, placement preparation materials, higher studies guidance, mentorship programs, resume reviews, mock interviews, coding contests, workshops, project-building opportunities, and industry interaction sessions.


In addition to learning and community activities, CodeMate helps students discover internship and freelance opportunities through its network, credibility, and industry connections. Students may also collaborate with core team members and fellow community members to develop projects, strengthen technical skills, and gain practical experience.


### Mission


The mission of CodeMate is to reduce the gap between students and career opportunities by providing resources, mentorship, technical exposure, project experience, and professional guidance that help members succeed in placements, higher studies, entrepreneurship, and the technology industry.


### Vision


The vision of CodeMate is to become the largest student-driven technology community in Northeast India, supported by a strong alumni network that enables mentorship, placements, startup incubation, industry collaboration, and long-term professional growth for its members.

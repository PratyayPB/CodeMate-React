## Directory Layout

All website image assets have been consolidated from chaotic locations into structured, semantic categories under `/public/images/`:

- `/public/images/logos/` — Brand marks and logos.
- `/public/images/icons/` — Favicons, web app icons, and framework vector graphics.
- `/public/images/heroes/` — Hero section reveal graphics and promotional banners.
- `/public/images/ui/` — UI graphic elements, illustrations, and campus location maps.
- `/public/images/alumni/` — Standardized portraits of alumni across global tech leaders, researchers, and founders.
- `/public/images/founders/` — CodeMate club co-founder profile portraits and thumbnail avatars.
- `/public/images/coordinators/` — University faculty teacher coordinator portraits and thumbnails.
- `/public/images/team/` — Active student leads, developers, designers, and community managers, plus the all-hands community group photograph.
- `/public/images/events/` — Event promotional banners, slides, and celebration photos organized by individual event subfolders:
  - `codefest-1/`
